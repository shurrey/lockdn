# Lockdn User Guide

Welcome to Lockdn — your private, local-first study companion. This guide helps you get the most out of Lockdn to organize your courses, manage your time, and succeed in your studies.

---

## What is Lockdn?

Lockdn is a free, open-source app that helps undergraduate students manage their academic lives. Unlike other study apps, Lockdn keeps all your data on your own device — nothing is stored on remote servers.

**Key benefits:**

- **Complete privacy** — Your notes, grades, and study habits stay on your device
- **AI-powered assistance** — Get help from an AI tutor that guides without giving answers
- **Smart scheduling** — Automatically plan study sessions based on your deadlines
- **Works offline** — Access your data anytime, even without internet
- **Sync across devices** — Securely share data between your own devices

## Features Overview

### Course Management
Add your courses, upload syllabi, and let Lockdn automatically extract all your assignments and due dates. Track your grades in real-time.

### Smart Dashboard
Your dashboard shows what needs your attention right now — upcoming deadlines, at-risk courses, and recommended study sessions.

### AI Tutor
Chat with an AI tutor that understands your courses. It guides you to answers rather than giving them directly, helping you actually learn the material.

### Study Planning
Tell Lockdn when you're most productive, and it creates an optimized study schedule based on your deadlines and workload.

### Note Processing
Upload photos of your handwritten notes. Lockdn extracts the text and organizes them by course.

### Study Materials
Generate study guides and practice exams from your notes. Take practice tests with AI-powered grading and feedback.

### Analytics
Understand your study habits with visualizations of your time, completion rates, and performance trends.

### Device Sync
Sync your data between your laptop, desktop, and other devices using peer-to-peer technology — no cloud storage required.

---

## Your Data, Your Control

Lockdn is built on a "local-first" philosophy:

- **No account required** — Start using Lockdn immediately
- **No cloud storage** — Your data lives in your browser's storage
- **No tracking** — We don't collect analytics about your usage
- **Bring your own key** — You provide your own AI API key, so conversations are between you and the AI provider
- **Export anytime** — Download all your data whenever you want
