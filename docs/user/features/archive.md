# Archive

The Archive is where your deleted and archived items go. You can restore items or permanently delete them from here.

---

## What gets archived

When you archive or delete items in Lockdn, they move to the Archive instead of being immediately deleted. This includes:

- **Courses** — Archived at semester end or manually
- **Assignments** — Archived when their course is archived
- **Notes** — Archived manually or with their course
- **Study materials** — Archived manually or with their course
- **Study sessions** — Archived with their course
- **Tutor conversations** — Archived manually or with their course

Archived items don't appear in your active views but aren't permanently deleted yet.

---

## Viewing archived items

1. Go to **Archive** from the main menu
2. See all archived items grouped by type
3. Use the **Filter** dropdown to show only specific types

Each item shows:

- **Title** — The item's name
- **Archive date** — When it was archived
- **Course** — Which course it belonged to (if applicable)
- **Reason** — Why it was archived (user action, course archived, semester cleanup)

---

## Restoring items

Bring an item back to your active data:

1. Find the item in the Archive
2. Click **Restore**
3. The item returns to its original location

> **Note:** If you restore an item whose parent course is still archived, the item will be restored but may not appear until you also restore the course.

---

## Permanent deletion

Remove items forever:

1. Find the item in the Archive
2. Click the trash icon
3. Confirm the permanent deletion

> **Warning:** Permanent deletion cannot be undone. The item and all its data are removed from your device completely.

---

## Semester archives

When you do a semester cleanup, Lockdn creates a semester archive that groups all the archived courses together.

### Viewing semester archives

Semester archives appear at the top of the Archive page. Each shows:

- **Semester name** — The name you gave the archive
- **Courses** — Which courses were included
- **Archive date** — When the cleanup was performed

### Deleting a semester archive

To permanently remove all data from a semester:

1. Find the semester archive at the top of the Archive page
2. Click **Delete Forever**
3. Confirm the action

This permanently deletes all courses, assignments, notes, and other data from that semester.

---

## Tips for managing archives

- **Review before permanent deletion** — Once deleted, data cannot be recovered
- **Restore before editing** — You can't edit archived items; restore them first
- **Archive at semester end** — Keep your active views clean by archiving completed semesters
- **Export before deleting** — If you might need the data later, export a backup first
