# Course Management

Courses are the foundation of Lockdn. This guide covers adding courses, managing assignments, and tracking your grades.

---

## Adding Courses

### From a Schedule Upload

The fastest way to add multiple courses:

1. Go to **Courses** → **Upload Schedule**
2. Select your class schedule (PDF, image, or screenshot)
3. Lockdn extracts course names and meeting times
4. Review and edit the extracted courses
5. Click **Confirm** to add them all

### Manually

1. Go to **Courses** → **Add Course**
2. Fill in the course details:
   - **Course name** (e.g., "Introduction to Psychology")
   - **Course code** (e.g., "PSY 101")
   - **Instructor** (optional)
   - **Color** — Choose a color to identify this course throughout Lockdn
3. Add meeting times (optional):
   - Select the day(s) of the week
   - Set the start and end times
   - Add the location (optional)
4. Click **Save**

## Uploading a Syllabus

Upload your syllabus to automatically extract assignments, exams, and due dates.

### Supported File Types

- PDF documents
- Word documents (.docx)
- Images (PNG, JPG, HEIC)
- Screenshots

### How to Upload

1. Open a course and click **Upload Syllabus**
2. Select your syllabus file
3. Wait for Lockdn to process the document

### Reviewing Extracted Items

After processing, Lockdn shows a list of extracted items:

- **Assignment title** — What Lockdn thinks this assignment is called
- **Type** — Exam, paper, homework, project, quiz, or other
- **Due date** — When Lockdn thinks this is due
- **Weight** — How much this counts toward your final grade
- **Confidence** — How certain Lockdn is about this extraction

> **Important:** Always review extracted items carefully. Check due dates against your syllabus, especially for items with low confidence scores.

### Editing Extracted Items

Click any extracted item to edit it before adding:

- Fix incorrect titles or dates
- Change the assignment type
- Adjust the weight percentage
- Add notes or details

### Selecting Items to Add

- Check the box next to items you want to add
- Uncheck items that are duplicates or errors
- Click **Add Selected Items** to save them to your course

## Managing Assignments

### Viewing Assignments

Open any course to see all its assignments. You can sort by:

- Due date (default)
- Status (pending, in progress, completed)
- Type (exams, papers, homework, etc.)
- Weight

### Adding Assignments Manually

1. Open the course
2. Click **Add Assignment**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — What's this assignment called?
   - **Type** — Exam, paper, homework, project, quiz, or other
   - **Due date and time** — When is it due?
   - **Weight** — What percentage of your grade? (e.g., 10%)
   - **Estimated effort** — How long do you think it'll take? (optional)
4. Click **Save**

### Editing Assignments

Click any assignment to open its details, then click **Edit** to make changes.

### Marking Assignments Complete

When you finish an assignment:

1. Click the assignment
2. Click **Mark Complete**
3. Enter your grade (if known) or leave it blank
4. Click **Save**

> **Tip:** Recording your grades helps Lockdn calculate your current standing in each course.

## Grade Tracking

Lockdn calculates your grade in real-time based on completed assignments and their weights.

### How Grade Calculation Works

Lockdn uses weighted average calculation:

- Each assignment has a weight (percentage of final grade)
- As you record grades, Lockdn calculates your current weighted average
- Assignments without grades are excluded from the calculation

### Viewing Your Grades

1. Open a course
2. Click **View Grades** or look at the grade summary at the top

You'll see:

- **Current grade** — Your grade based on completed work
- **Grade breakdown** — How each assignment contributes
- **Remaining weight** — What percentage of your grade is still ungraded

### Recording Grades

When you get a grade back:

1. Open the assignment
2. Click **Edit** or **Record Grade**
3. Enter your score (0-100)
4. Click **Save**

## Course Settings

### Changing Course Color

The course color appears throughout Lockdn — on the calendar, dashboard, and charts.

1. Open the course
2. Click **Edit Course**
3. Select a new color
4. Click **Save**

### Setting Semester Dates

Track when your course starts and ends:

1. Open the course
2. Click **Edit Course**
3. Set the **Start Date** and **End Date**
4. Click **Save**

This helps with study planning and archiving old courses.

## Archiving Courses

At the end of a semester, archive completed courses to keep your active course list clean.

### How to Archive

1. Go to **Settings** → **Data Management** → **Archive**
2. Select the courses to archive
3. Add an archive reason (e.g., "Fall 2024 Semester")
4. Click **Archive Selected**

### What Gets Archived

Archiving moves all related data:

- The course itself
- All assignments
- Associated notes
- Study materials
- Completed study sessions

### Accessing Archived Data

Archived courses aren't deleted — they're just hidden from your main view.

1. Go to **Archive** in the main menu
2. Browse your archived semesters
3. Click any item to view its details

> **Note:** Archived items are read-only. To make changes, restore the course first.

## Tips for Success

- **Upload syllabi early** — Get all your deadlines into Lockdn during the first week
- **Review extraction carefully** — Spend time verifying due dates; it saves headaches later
- **Record grades promptly** — Keeping grades current helps you track your standing
- **Use course colors wisely** — Pick distinct colors so courses are easy to identify
- **Archive at semester end** — Start each semester with a clean slate
