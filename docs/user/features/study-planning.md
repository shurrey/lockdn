# Study Planning

Lockdn creates personalized study schedules based on your deadlines, workload, and when you study best. This guide explains how to use the study planner effectively.

---

## How Study Planning Works

Lockdn analyzes several factors to create your study plan:

1. **Your deadlines** — When assignments are due
2. **Assignment weights** — Higher-weight assignments get more study time
3. **Your productivity hours** — When you've told Lockdn you study best
4. **Estimated effort** — How long each assignment might take
5. **Your history** — Patterns from your past study sessions (over time)

The result is a personalized schedule that helps you prepare for everything without cramming.

---

## Setting Up Your Preferences

Before generating a study plan, tell Lockdn when you study best.

### Productivity Hours

1. Go to **Settings** → **Study Preferences**
2. For each day of the week, mark your best study times:
   - **Morning** (6 AM - 12 PM)
   - **Afternoon** (12 PM - 6 PM)
   - **Evening** (6 PM - 12 AM)
3. Set your energy level for each time block:
   - **High** — Best for difficult material
   - **Medium** — Good for regular studying
   - **Low** — Better for review or easy tasks
4. Click **Save**

### Break Preferences

Set how often you want breaks:

- **Break frequency** — How long to study before a break (e.g., 50 minutes)
- **Break duration** — How long each break should be (e.g., 10 minutes)

This follows the Pomodoro technique pattern if you prefer structured intervals.

## Generating a Study Plan

### Creating Your Plan

1. Go to **Study** from the main menu
2. Click **Generate Study Plan**
3. Lockdn analyzes your upcoming deadlines
4. Review the suggested study sessions
5. Click **Accept Plan** to add sessions to your calendar

### What's in the Plan

Each study session includes:

- **Course and topic** — What you'll study
- **Suggested time** — When to study (based on your preferences)
- **Duration** — How long to study
- **Related materials** — Links to relevant notes

### Adjusting the Plan

Not every suggestion will fit your schedule. You can:

- **Reschedule** — Drag sessions to different times
- **Skip** — Remove sessions you don't need
- **Add manually** — Create sessions for additional study time

## Working with Study Sessions

### Starting a Session

When it's time to study:

1. Open the study session from your calendar or dashboard
2. Click **Start Session**
3. Lockdn tracks how long you study
4. Access related notes and materials from the session view

### During Your Session

While studying, you can:

- **View related notes** — See notes linked to this topic
- **Open the tutor** — Ask questions about the material
- **Generate practice questions** — Test yourself on the content

### Ending a Session

When you're done:

1. Click **End Session**
2. Lockdn records your actual study time
3. Your analytics update automatically

> **Tip:** Even if you study more or less than planned, recording accurate times helps Lockdn learn your patterns.

---

## Handling Missed Sessions

Life happens. When you miss a planned study session:

### Automatic Detection

Lockdn notices when planned sessions pass without being completed and shows them on your dashboard.

### Rescheduling Options

When you view a missed session:

- **Reschedule** — Move it to a new time
- **Skip** — Remove it if no longer needed
- **Mark Complete** — If you actually completed it elsewhere

### Smart Rescheduling

Lockdn suggests new times based on:

- Your remaining productive hours
- Proximity to the deadline
- Other scheduled sessions

## Plan Adjustments

Your study plan isn't static. Lockdn adjusts when things change.

### When Plans Update

- **New assignment added** — Plan recalculates priorities
- **Due date changed** — Sessions may shift earlier or later
- **Session missed** — Time reallocates to remaining sessions

### Rebalancing

If you fall behind or get ahead:

1. Go to **Study** → **Rebalance Plan**
2. Lockdn recalculates based on current state
3. Review the updated suggestions
4. Accept or modify the new plan

## Understanding Recommendations

### Priority Indicators

Study sessions show priority levels:

- **🔴 Critical** — Due very soon, high-weight assignment
- **🟡 High** — Approaching deadline or important
- **🟢 Normal** — Standard study session
- **⚪ Optional** — Review or extra practice

### Time Estimates

Lockdn estimates study time based on:

- Assignment type (exams need more prep than homework)
- Weight in your grade
- Your past performance on similar tasks
- Time until deadline

> **Note:** Estimates improve as Lockdn learns from your actual study patterns.

## Study Patterns and Insights

Over time, Lockdn learns how you study.

### What Lockdn Tracks

- **Actual vs. planned time** — Do you study longer or shorter than planned?
- **Time of day** — When are your most productive sessions?
- **Day of week** — Which days work best for you?
- **Session length** — What's your optimal study duration?

### How This Helps

These insights improve:

- Future study plan suggestions
- More accurate time estimates
- Better scheduling around your peak times

### Viewing Your Patterns

Go to **Analytics** to see visualizations of your study habits.

## Tips for Effective Study Planning

### Getting Started

- **Set accurate preferences** — Be honest about when you actually study
- **Start with one course** — Get comfortable with the system
- **Review generated plans** — Don't blindly accept; make adjustments

### Building Habits

- **Stick to the schedule** — Consistency helps Lockdn learn
- **Record accurate times** — Even if you deviate from the plan
- **Use the tutor** — Get help when stuck during sessions

### Optimizing Over Time

- **Check analytics weekly** — Notice patterns in your productivity
- **Adjust preferences** — Update if your schedule changes
- **Rebalance after exams** — Reset priorities when big deadlines pass

## Common Questions

### "Why isn't my plan showing study time for a course?"

Check that:
- The course has assignments with due dates
- Due dates are in the future
- You have productivity hours set for upcoming days

### "Can I plan for assignments without due dates?"

Yes! Add an estimated due date when creating the assignment. You can always update it later.

### "How far ahead does the planner look?"

By default, Lockdn plans for the next 2 weeks. Major assignments (exams, papers) may appear earlier.

### "Can I study without a plan?"

Absolutely. The planner is a guide, not a requirement. Study whenever works for you.
