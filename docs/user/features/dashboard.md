# Dashboard

The dashboard is your home base in Lockdn. It shows what needs your attention, upcoming deadlines, and quick access to common actions.

---

## What needs attention

The Attention Card at the top of your dashboard highlights items that need your focus right now. Lockdn prioritizes these intelligently based on:

- **Deadlines approaching** — Assignments due soon appear first
- **Missing grades** — Completed assignments without recorded grades
- **Study streaks** — Courses you haven't studied recently
- **Unprocessed materials** — Notes or materials waiting for review

Click any item to jump directly to it.

---

## Upcoming deadlines

This widget shows your next five assignments across all courses, sorted by due date.

### Marking assignments complete

You can mark assignments complete directly from the dashboard:

1. Click the checkmark icon next to any assignment
2. Enter your grade (optional)
3. Click **Save**

Click **View Calendar** to see all your deadlines in calendar view.

---

## Today's study plan

This widget shows either:

- **Planned sessions** — Study sessions you've scheduled for today
- **Recommendations** — If you haven't planned anything, Lockdn suggests what to focus on based on upcoming deadlines and assignment weights

Click **Plan Study Session** to create a detailed study schedule.

---

## Quick actions

Common tasks you can start with one click:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| Import Schedule | Upload your class schedule to add multiple courses at once |
| Add Course | Manually create a new course |
| Upload Notes | Add notes to organize and study from |
| Ask Tutor | Start a conversation with your AI study buddy |

> **Note:** Import Schedule and Ask Tutor require an API key. Configure one in Settings if these buttons are disabled.

---

## Your courses

A quick view of your active courses. Each course shows:

- **Course code and name**
- **Study streak** — Days in a row you've studied this course (shown as a flame icon)

Click **Manage Courses** to add, edit, or archive courses.

---

## Study streak

Your overall study streak tracks consecutive days where you've logged study time. The widget shows:

- **Current streak** — How many days in a row you've studied
- **Longest streak** — Your personal best

Building a study streak helps establish consistent study habits.

---

## AI Tutor

Quick access to your AI study buddy. The tutor can help with:

- Explaining difficult concepts
- Creating practice problems
- Reviewing your notes
- Answering questions about your course material

If you haven't configured an API key yet, this widget will prompt you to set one up in Settings.

---

## First-time setup

If you're new to Lockdn, the dashboard shows a getting started checklist:

1. **Configure AI provider** — Add your API key to enable AI features
2. **Add your courses** — Import your schedule or add courses manually
3. **Upload syllabi** — Let Lockdn extract assignments and deadlines automatically

Complete these steps to unlock all of Lockdn's features.

---

## Tips for your dashboard

- **Check your dashboard daily** — The Attention Card shows what's most urgent
- **Use quick actions** — They're faster than navigating through menus
- **Keep streaks going** — Even 15 minutes of study maintains your streak
- **Record grades promptly** — This helps Lockdn track your progress accurately
