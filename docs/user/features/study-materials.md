# Study Materials

Generate study guides and practice exams from your notes. This guide covers creating, using, and managing study materials.

---

## Types of Study Materials

Lockdn generates two types of study materials:

### Study Guides

Structured summaries that help you review:

- Key term definitions
- Concept explanations
- Important relationships
- Summary of main ideas

### Practice Exams

Test yourself with AI-generated questions:

- Multiple choice
- Fill in the blank
- Matching
- Free response

---

## Generating Study Guides

### From a Single Note

1. Open any note
2. Click **Generate Study Guide**
3. Wait for generation (30-60 seconds)
4. Review your new study guide

### From Multiple Notes

Combine several notes into one comprehensive guide:

1. Go to **Study Materials**
2. Click **Generate Study Guide**
3. Select the notes to include
4. Click **Generate**

### Study Guide Contents

A generated study guide includes:

- **Overview** — Summary of the topic
- **Key Terms** — Important vocabulary with definitions
- **Main Concepts** — Core ideas explained
- **Relationships** — How concepts connect
- **Summary** — Quick review of key points

## Generating Practice Exams

### Creating a Practice Exam

1. Go to **Study Materials**
2. Click **Generate Practice Exam**
3. Select the notes to base questions on
4. Choose question types:
   - Multiple choice
   - Fill in the blank
   - Matching
   - Free response
5. Set the number of questions
6. Click **Generate**

### Question Difficulty

Questions are tagged by Bloom's taxonomy level:

- **Remember** — Recall facts and basic concepts
- **Understand** — Explain ideas or concepts
- **Apply** — Use information in new situations
- **Analyze** — Draw connections among ideas
- **Evaluate** — Justify decisions or judgments
- **Create** — Produce new or original work

Higher levels are more challenging.

## Taking Practice Exams

### Starting an Exam

1. Open a practice exam
2. Click **Start Exam**
3. Answer each question
4. Click **Submit** when done

### During the Exam

- Navigate between questions freely
- Mark questions to review later
- See your progress (questions answered)
- No time limit (unless you set one)

### Question Types

**Multiple Choice**
- Select the best answer
- Immediately see if correct after submitting

**Fill in the Blank**
- Type your answer
- Flexible grading (allows minor typos)

**Matching**
- Connect items from two columns
- Drag and drop or select matches

**Free Response**
- Write your answer in a text box
- AI grades and provides feedback

---

## Exam Grading

### Automatic Grading

Most question types are graded automatically:

- **Multiple choice** — Exact match required
- **Fill in the blank** — Allows minor spelling variations
- **Matching** — All pairs must be correct

### AI Grading for Free Response

Free response questions use AI grading:

- Scored on a 0-10 scale
- Detailed feedback on your answer
- Partial credit for partially correct answers
- Suggestions for improvement

### Viewing Results

After submitting:

1. See your overall score
2. Review each question
3. View correct answers
4. Read feedback on wrong answers

---

## Retaking Exams

### Unlimited Attempts

You can take practice exams as many times as you want:

- Each attempt is recorded
- Track improvement over time
- Questions remain the same (test knowledge, not luck)

### Viewing Attempt History

1. Open the practice exam
2. Click **View Attempts**
3. See all previous attempts with scores
4. Click any attempt to review answers

### Regenerating Questions

Want fresh questions?

1. Open the practice exam
2. Click **Regenerate Questions**
3. Get a new set based on the same notes

## Managing Study Materials

### Viewing Your Materials

Go to **Study Materials** to see all generated content:

- Filter by course
- Filter by type (guides vs. exams)
- Sort by date or course

### Organizing Materials

Each study material shows:

- **Title** — Auto-generated or custom
- **Course** — Which course it's for
- **Source notes** — Which notes it came from
- **Created date** — When you generated it

### Editing Titles

1. Open the study material
2. Click **Edit**
3. Change the title
4. Click **Save**

### Deleting Materials

1. Open the study material
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm deletion

> **Note:** Deleting study materials doesn't affect the source notes.

## Using Materials Effectively

### Study Guide Strategies

- **Read actively** — Don't just skim; engage with the content
- **Test yourself** — Cover definitions and try to recall them
- **Make connections** — Link concepts to things you already know
- **Review regularly** — Spaced repetition beats cramming

### Practice Exam Strategies

- **Take exams closed-book first** — Test actual recall
- **Review wrong answers** — Understand why you missed them
- **Retake until confident** — Aim for consistent high scores
- **Time yourself** — Build exam-taking speed

### Combining Materials

- Generate study guide → Read and understand
- Generate practice exam → Test yourself
- Review weak areas → Return to notes or tutor
- Retake exam → Confirm understanding

## Tips for Best Results

### Generating Quality Materials

- **Use detailed notes** — More content = better materials
- **Include multiple notes** — Broader coverage of topics
- **Recent notes work best** — While context is fresh
- **Verify accuracy** — Check generated content against source

### Getting Better Questions

Practice exam quality depends on:

- Note detail and coverage
- Clear topic boundaries
- Variety in note content
- Specific vs. general information

### Improving Over Time

Track your practice exam scores:

- Notice patterns in what you miss
- Focus study on weak areas
- Retake exams after additional study
- Watch scores improve

## Troubleshooting

### "Study guide is too short"

- Include more notes as source material
- Ensure notes have substantial content
- Try uploading more detailed notes

### "Questions seem off-topic"

- Check that source notes cover intended topic
- Generate a new exam with different notes
- Edit note titles to clarify content

### "Free response grading seems wrong"

- AI grading isn't perfect
- Use it as guidance, not absolute truth
- Focus on the feedback, not just the score

### "Generation is taking too long"

- Large note sets take longer
- Check your internet connection
- Try with fewer notes first
