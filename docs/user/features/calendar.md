# Calendar

The calendar gives you a visual overview of your deadlines, classes, and study sessions. This guide covers navigating and using the calendar effectively.

---

## Accessing the Calendar

Click **Calendar** in the main menu to open your academic calendar.

---

## Calendar Views

### Month View

See the entire month at a glance:

- Assignment due dates
- Class meeting times
- Scheduled study sessions
- Completed items (grayed out)

Best for: Planning ahead, seeing the big picture

### Week View

Detailed view of one week:

- Time blocks for each day
- Class schedules
- Study session durations
- Overlapping events visible

Best for: Weekly planning, seeing daily detail

### Day View

Focus on a single day:

- Hour-by-hour schedule
- All events for that day
- Most detail about timing

Best for: Daily planning, tight schedules

### Agenda View

List of upcoming items:

- Chronological list
- Next 30 days
- Easy scanning

Best for: Quick overview of what's coming

---

## What's on the Calendar

### Assignments

- Show on their due dates
- Color-coded by course
- Click to see details or mark complete

### Class Meetings

- Recurring events for your class schedule
- Show day and time
- Color-coded by course

### Study Sessions

- Planned study blocks
- Show duration
- Links to related course
- Click to start session

---

## Color Coding

Each course has a unique color:

- Assignments appear in their course color
- Class meetings use the same color
- Study sessions match the course
- Easy visual identification

To change a course color:
1. Go to **Courses**
2. Edit the course
3. Select a new color

---

## Navigating the Calendar

### Moving Through Time

- **Today button** — Jump to current day
- **← → arrows** — Move forward/back by current view period
- **Date picker** — Jump to specific date

### Switching Views

Buttons at the top switch between:
- Month / Week / Day / Agenda

### Quick Navigation

- Click any date in month view to see that day
- Double-click a date to switch to day view

---

## Adding Events

### Adding Assignments

1. Click on a date
2. Select **Add Assignment**
3. Fill in details
4. Click **Save**

Or:
1. Go to **Courses**
2. Select a course
3. Click **Add Assignment**

### Adding Study Sessions

1. Click on a date
2. Select **Add Study Session**
3. Choose course and duration
4. Click **Save**

Or: Generate study sessions through the Study Planner.

---

## Managing Events

### Viewing Event Details

Click any event to see:

- Full title and description
- Time and duration
- Course information
- Related actions

### Editing Events

From the event detail popup:

1. Click **Edit**
2. Make changes
3. Click **Save**

### Deleting Events

From the event detail popup:

1. Click **Delete**
2. Confirm deletion

### Rescheduling

**Drag and drop** — Drag events to new dates/times
**Edit** — Open and change the date manually

---

## Filtering the Calendar

### By Course

See events for specific courses:

1. Click **Filter** (filter icon)
2. Select courses to show
3. Unselected courses are hidden

### By Event Type

Show only certain types:

- Assignments
- Study sessions
- Class meetings

## Calendar Integration Tips

### Planning Your Week

1. Open week view on Sunday or Monday
2. Review all deadlines for the week
3. Identify heavy days
4. Plan study sessions around deadlines
5. Account for class times

### Avoiding Conflicts

The calendar helps you spot:

- Multiple deadlines on one day
- Study sessions during class
- Overbooked days

### Using with Study Planner

Study sessions from the planner appear automatically:

1. Generate a study plan
2. Accept the suggested sessions
3. They appear on your calendar
4. Adjust by dragging to new times

## Troubleshooting

### "Events aren't showing"

Check:
- The course filter isn't hiding them
- Event types aren't filtered out
- The date range is correct

### "Can't drag events"

Dragging works in week and day views. In month view, click to edit instead.

### "Calendar is slow"

With many events, try:
- Filtering to fewer courses
- Using week view instead of month
- Archive old semester data

### "Class times are wrong"

Edit the course:
1. Go to **Courses**
2. Select the course
3. Click **Edit**
4. Update meeting times
5. Click **Save**
