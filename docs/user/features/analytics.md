# Analytics

Lockdn tracks your study habits and academic progress. This guide explains how to understand and use your analytics.

---

## Overview

Analytics help you:

- Understand your study patterns
- Track progress across courses
- Identify what's working (and what isn't)
- Celebrate wins and adjust strategies

All analytics are calculated from your local data — nothing is sent anywhere.

---

## Accessing Analytics

Go to **Analytics** in the main menu to see your personal insights.

---

## Study Time Analytics

### Time Distribution

See how your study time breaks down:

- **By course** — Which courses get the most attention
- **By day** — Which days you study most
- **By time of day** — Your most active hours

### Weekly Trends

Compare your study time week over week:

- Total hours studied
- Change from last week
- Sessions completed

### Daily Breakdown

View study time for specific days:

- Hours per course
- Number of sessions
- Completed vs. planned time

---

## Productivity Insights

### Productivity Heatmap

A 90-day view showing when you study most:

- Days are columns
- Hours are rows
- Darker = more study time

**How to use this:**
- Notice your natural patterns
- Schedule important study during peak times
- Identify days you tend to skip

### Planned vs. Actual Time

See how well you stick to your study plans:

- **Planned time** — What you scheduled
- **Actual time** — What you recorded
- **Difference** — Over or under

This helps calibrate future time estimates.

### Session Duration

Understand your typical study session:

- Average session length
- Most common duration
- Longest and shortest sessions

## Academic Performance

### Grade Tracking

For each course, see:

- **Current grade** — Based on recorded assignments
- **Trend** — Improving, stable, or declining
- **Grade breakdown** — How each assignment contributed

### Completion Rates

Track assignment completion:

- **On-time rate** — Percentage submitted by deadline
- **Late submissions** — Assignments submitted after due date
- **Missing** — Past-due assignments without grades

### Course Performance Cards

Each course gets a performance summary:

- Current grade
- Assignment completion percentage
- Study time invested
- Performance trend

---

## Streaks

### What Streaks Track

- **Study streak** — Consecutive days with study sessions
- **Per-course streaks** — Consistency within each course

### Viewing Streaks

Your dashboard and analytics show:

- Current streak length
- Longest streak ever
- Streak status (active, at risk, broken)

### Streak at Risk

If you haven't studied today and your streak is active, Lockdn shows a gentle reminder.

## Understanding Your Data

### What's Tracked

Lockdn automatically tracks:

- Study session start and end times
- Course associations
- Assignment completion and grades
- Practice exam attempts and scores

### What's Not Tracked

Lockdn never tracks:

- Your location
- What websites you visit
- Idle time during sessions
- Content of your notes or conversations

### Data Freshness

Analytics update when you:

- Complete a study session
- Record a grade
- Finish a practice exam

## Using Analytics Effectively

### Daily Check-In

Spend 30 seconds reviewing:

- Did you meet yesterday's study goals?
- Are you on track for upcoming deadlines?
- Is your streak intact?

### Weekly Review

Once a week, look at:

- Total study time vs. previous week
- Course balance (are you neglecting any?)
- Grade trends
- Upcoming heavy periods

### End-of-Semester Review

Before archiving:

- Final grades vs. study time invested
- Most and least effective study patterns
- Lessons for next semester

---

## Tips for Better Analytics

### Record Accurate Data

Analytics are only as good as your input:

- Start and end study sessions accurately
- Record grades when you receive them
- Don't fabricate data (it only hurts you)

### Use Analytics to Adjust

Don't just view analytics — act on them:

- Struggling course → More study time
- Broken streak → Recommit tomorrow
- Declining grades → Talk to the tutor

### Set Personal Benchmarks

Define what success looks like for you:

- Target study hours per week
- Minimum streak length
- Grade goals per course

## Troubleshooting

### "Analytics seem incomplete"

- Check that you're recording study sessions
- Ensure grades are entered for completed work
- Some analytics need a week of data to appear

### "Grades don't match my calculations"

- Verify assignment weights are correct
- Check that all graded work is recorded
- Confirm no assignments are missing grades

### "Heatmap is empty"

The productivity heatmap requires:

- At least a few recorded study sessions
- Sessions from the past 90 days

### "Streak reset unexpectedly"

Streaks require study sessions every calendar day. If you studied late at night, ensure it was before midnight in your timezone.
