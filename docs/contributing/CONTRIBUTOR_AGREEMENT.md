## Common Questions

### Why is this necessary?

Open source projects need clear licensing to protect both contributors and users. This agreement ensures that:

- Contributions can be freely used and distributed
- The project has necessary rights to include your work
- Users can rely on the open source license

### What rights do I retain?

You retain all rights to your contribution. This agreement grants a license to use your contribution in Lockdn, but you can also use your code in other projects, license it differently elsewhere, or do anything else you want with it.

### What if I change my mind?

Once submitted, contributions cannot be withdrawn because users and other contributors may have come to rely on them. However, you can request your name be removed from attribution.

### What about contributions from AI assistants?

If you use AI coding assistants (GitHub Copilot, Claude, etc.), you are responsible for ensuring the generated code doesn't infringe on others' rights. By submitting, you represent that you have reviewed the code and have the right to contribute it.

### What if my employer owns my code?

Many employment contracts assign intellectual property rights to employers. If you're uncertain, check with your employer before contributing. You may need written permission.

### What if I'm a minor?

If you are under 18, please have a parent or legal guardian review this agreement before contributing.
